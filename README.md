# 👥 Employee Attrition & Workforce Demographics Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce demographics using HR employee data.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in employee attrition across departments, business travel frequency, age groups, DailyRate quartiles, and education fields.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Measure overall employee attrition
* Compare attrition across departments
* Analyze attrition by business travel frequency
* Study attrition across different age groups
* Examine attrition by DailyRate quartiles
* Compare attrition across education fields
* Analyze workforce demographic distributions
* Identify patterns that may be useful for employee retention analysis

## 📌 Dataset

The dataset contains information about **1,470 employees** and **10 selected attributes**.

Key variables include:

* Age
* Attrition
* Business Travel
* Daily Rate
* Department
* Education
* Education Field
* Employee Number
* Distance From Home
* Gender

## 🔍 Data Quality

The analysis found:

* **1,470 employee records**
* **10 columns**
* **0 missing values**
* **0 duplicate rows**
* **0 duplicate Employee Numbers**

## 📈 Key Findings

### Overall Attrition

* Employees who left: **237**
* Employees who stayed: **1,233**
* Observed attrition rate: **16.12%**

### Attrition by Department

* Sales: **20.63%**
* Human Resources: **19.05%**
* Research & Development: **13.84%**

### Attrition by Business Travel

* Travel Frequently: **24.91%**
* Travel Rarely: **14.96%**
* Non-Travel: **8.00%**

### Attrition by Age Group

* Under 26: **35.77%**
* 26–35: **19.14%**
* 36–45: **9.19%**
* 46–55: **11.50%**
* 56+: **17.02%**

## 💡 Business Relevance

The analysis demonstrates how HR data can be used to identify workforce patterns and areas that may require further investigation.

The findings can support deeper analysis of employee retention, workforce planning, and HR decision-making.

These results should be interpreted as **observed patterns in this dataset**, not as proof that any individual factor directly causes employee attrition.

## ⚠️ Limitations

The dataset contains only selected employee attributes. Important variables such as salary, job role, overtime, job satisfaction, tenure, promotions, and performance ratings were not included in this analysis.

Therefore, the observed relationships should not be interpreted as causal conclusions.
